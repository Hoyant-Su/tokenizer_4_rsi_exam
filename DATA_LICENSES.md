## Redistribution

WebNLG includes noncommercial and ShareAlike restrictions; CodeXGLUE uses the Computational Use of Data Agreement. Other sources have the individual attribution and release conditions listed above. This collection is not offered under a single unrestricted data license. Resolve pending permissions before public redistribution.
