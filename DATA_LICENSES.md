## Redistribution

ESOL and the original Matbench experimental data have noncommercial restrictions. WebNLG also requires ShareAlike; ChEMBL, DS-1000 and Spider have their respective ShareAlike conditions. CodeXGLUE uses the Computational Use of Data Agreement. Redistribution is subject to each dataset's listed terms; this collection is not offered under one unrestricted data license.
